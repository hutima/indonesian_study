# Textbook topic map

Reference: Ulrich Kozok, *Yang Tersirat dan Yang Tersurat*, Student Book 1, the fifteen Topik PDFs supplied by the learner. The app follows the book's topic sequence and selects words and grammar patterns to practice. Its sample sentences, short reading passages, and comprehension questions are original. Do not add the supplied PDFs, source articles, photographs, exercises, or answer keys to the repository.

The book's authentic media texts are denser than the original foundation lessons. Each Topik therefore offers 21–30 selected vocabulary cards, three or four contextual morphology drills, and one or two short original passages with comprehension questions. Select multiple topics to practice a combined deck, or use the earlier foundation units independently. This is a curated study companion, not a complete reproduction of each glossary.

When extending a topic, retain stable item IDs so saved practice stays attached to the same word or question. Keep register, part of speech, base/root, and the meaning of an affix in context visible to the learner.
